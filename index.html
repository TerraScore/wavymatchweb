Feature 03 · Spots & Events

Show up. See who's already there.

Create a location-based Spot and give it a name (call it Thirdwave, we won't judge). Everyone who joins can see each other and start talking. Make it public so anyone nearby can hop in, or private and approve people yourself, like a bouncer but nicer. Spots are always-on hangouts: a cafe, a park, a co-working space. Events are scheduled and sometimes ticketed: a badminton mixer, a movie night, hosted by someone who actually got around to organizing something.

  • Always on. No countdown, no expiry, your Spot stays live for as long as you want it.
  • Public or private. Open the door to everyone nearby, or hand-pick who gets in.
  • Everyone connects. Anyone in the Spot can see who else is there on the map and start a chat.
  • Hosted & ticketed. Anyone can + Host an Event, free or paid, and see who's coming before it starts (so you know if it's worth putting on real pants).
